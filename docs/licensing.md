# Licensing Notes

EvOS is distributed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).

Why MPL?

Because we believe software should be free to use, study, modify, and distribute.

We welcome commercial use.

We welcome businesses built around EvOS.

We welcome experimentation, research, education, and innovation.

At the same time, we believe it is fair that improvements made directly to EvOS remain available to the wider community.

MPL-2.0 reflects this balance.

It allows both open and commercial development while helping ensure that modifications to EvOS itself remain part of the shared ecosystem.

The license defines the legal rules.

Our philosophy is described separately in `docs/philosophy.md`.
